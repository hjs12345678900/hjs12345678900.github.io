About / 关于我

From cosmic populations
to single-frame events.

My research asks how supermassive black holes assemble across cosmic time. Alongside that long-timescale view, I reduce quasar imaging data and observe occultations—brief moments in which geometry turns light into a precise measurement. Astrophotography keeps the same questions personal.
