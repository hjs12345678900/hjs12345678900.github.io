03 Fast astronomy

Occultations.
Timing the shadow.

An occultation can last seconds—or less. It turns a carefully predicted alignment into a direct measurement of size, shape, position, and atmosphere.

✦

At the telescope

Preparation is most of the observation.

My occultation work spans event assessment, path and field planning, equipment setup, high-speed acquisition, and light-curve analysis. The same discipline applies to asteroid and artificial-satellite events: know the geometry, keep the timing honest, and document every assumption before the shadow arrives.

SatOccult

An independently maintained planning and search tool for satellite occultations. It identifies close approaches to catalogue stars, refines contact times, supports ground-site planning, and exports observing-ready event results.
