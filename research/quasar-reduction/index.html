02 Observational pipeline

Quasar data
reduction.

A detector records photons and systematics together. Reduction is the work of telling them apart—carefully enough that the faint structure remains believable.

The work

From raw near-infrared frames to calibrated science images.

I work with quasar imaging data and reproducible reduction workflows, including detector calibration, sky subtraction, registration, combination, and quality control. For compact sources, the point-spread function is part of the science: comparison-star modelling and shifted, brightness-scaled subtraction help expose unresolved or extended residuals.

02

Subtract

04

Interrogate

PSF subtraction, residual maps, profiles, and diagnostics.

Current emphasis

Residuals are evidence only when the subtraction is understood.

My analysis treats centring, brightness scaling, comparison-star choice, and the radial behaviour of the residual as a coupled problem. The aim is not merely a clean image, but a transparent chain from raw data to astrophysical interpretation.
